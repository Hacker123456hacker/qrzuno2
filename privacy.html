Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. This page explains, in plain language, what happens to your data when you use QRZuno.

QR generation happens in your browser

Basic QR code generation runs entirely client-side. The text, link, Wi-Fi details, or contact information you enter is turned into a QR code on your own device — it is not sent to a QRZuno server to be processed.

Local history and favorites

Your recent QR codes and any favorites you save are stored using your browser's local storage, on your device only. QRZuno has no way to see or retrieve this data, and it is never uploaded.

No advertising

QRZuno contains no advertising system of any kind. It does not use Google AdSense, ad networks, or ad-tracking scripts.

No sale of data

QRZuno does not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties, because it does not collect QR content in the first place.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, the information you submit is used only to respond to your message.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will reflect it.
