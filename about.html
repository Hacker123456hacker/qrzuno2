About QRZuno

QRZuno is a free QR code generator built for anyone who needs a QR code without friction — no account, no ads, no hidden paywall for basic use. Type in your link, contact card, Wi-Fi details or payment information, watch the preview update instantly, and download it.

Why we built it

Most QR generators bury a simple task under ad networks, forced sign-ups, or watermarks on the free tier. QRZuno's basic generation runs entirely in your browser — nothing about the QR code you're creating needs to touch a server, so it doesn't.

What makes QRZuno different

  • Client-side generation for every core QR type
  • Full customization — colors, gradients, patterns, logos and frames
  • Local-only history and favorites, stored in your browser
  • No advertising of any kind, anywhere on the site
  • Works offline once loaded, as an installable PWA

Who it's for

Small businesses printing table QR codes, developers linking to app stores, event organizers sharing Wi-Fi, and anyone who just needs a quick, clean QR code without the clutter.
