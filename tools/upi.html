UPI Payment QR Code Generator

A UPI QR code encodes a payment request that UPI apps (like Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm) can read to pre-fill a transfer.

What is a UPI QR code?

It's built from a upi://pay link containing your UPI ID, name and optionally an amount and note.

How to create one

Enter your UPI ID and payee name; amount and note are optional. QRZuno only builds the QR payload — it never touches the money.

How to scan it

Any UPI-enabled banking or payment app can scan the code to pre-fill a payment.

Common uses

Shop counters, invoices, donation drives, and peer-to-peer requests.
