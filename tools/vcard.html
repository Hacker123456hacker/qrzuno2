vCard / Contact QR Code Generator

A vCard QR code stores a complete digital business card that phones can save straight to contacts.

What is a vCard QR code?

It encodes contact details in the standard vCard format, which most phones offer to save directly.

How to create one

Fill in your name, company, phone, email, website and address — only your first name is required.

How to scan it

Scanning prompts the phone to add a new contact with the details already filled in.

Common uses

Digital business cards, networking events, and email signatures.
